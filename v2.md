To step up from V1's direct receipting to a production-grade Version 2 Cashbook Reconciliation Framework, ThorCash must bridge the gap between two completely separate realities: the school’s internal Double-Entry General Ledger and the external National Bank Statements.
Because Friends School Kikai Boys operates a strict "no cash" policy, the Cashbook is the absolute core of the school's audit trail. Here is the operational design and execution framework for the V2 module.
------------------------------
## 1. The V2 Cashbook Architecture
In Version 2, the Cashbook is divided into two operational sub-ledgers under the asset control account (1010 - National Bank of Kenya - Bungoma Branch):

* The Cashbook Debit Ledger (Receipting / Inflow): Fed directly by the ReceiptAllocationEngine when a bank pay-in slip or M-Pesa transaction is posted.
* The Cashbook Credit Ledger (Vouchers / Outflow): Fed by the newly introduced Payment Vouchers (PV) system when the school spends money on food, utilities, or BOM salaries.

## The Reconciliation State Model
Every transaction entering the Cashbook starts as Unreconciled. It can only change state via the reconciliation workflow:

                  ┌──────────────────────────────┐
                  │  Unreconciled Cashbook Entry │
                  └──────────────┬───────────────┘
                                 │
                 [Matched against Bank Statement]
                                 │
                                 ▼
                  ┌──────────────────────────────┐
                  │   Reconciled (Locked State)  │
                  └──────────────────────────────┘

## 2. The Core Problems V2 Reconciliation Solves
A naive system assumes the bank balance matches the cashbook balance. In a real secondary school, they never match. ThorCash V2 handles the three structural anomalies that break school accounts:
## A. Unpresented Cheques (Outflow Discrepancy)

* The Scenario: The Principal signs a voucher and issues a cheque of KSh 50,000 to a supplier for maize delivery on June 15th. The Bursar records this in the Cashbook immediately. However, the supplier stores the cheque in their desk and doesn't cash it until July 10th.
* The V2 Fix: The Cashbook balance drops by KSh 50,000 immediately, but the Bank Statement balance remains high. The reconciliation module leaves this entry marked as Unpresented. When generating the end-of-month report, ThorCash automatically calculates:
$$\text{Adjusted Bank Balance} = \text{Statement Balance} - \text{Unpresented Cheques}$$ 

## B. Direct Bank Credits & Direct Debits (Inflow/Outflow Discrepancy)

* The Scenario: A parent deposits KSh 20,000 directly into the Bungoma Branch account via an agent but loses the slip and fails to bring it to the school. Alternatively, the bank deducts KSh 1,200 for ledger fees at midnight. Neither is in the school's Cashbook.
* The V2 Fix: During statement reconciliation, the system provides a split screen. Unmatched bank entries appear on the right side. The Bursar can click "Generate Receipt from Statement" (for the parent's fee) or "Post Bank Charges Voucher" (for the ledger fees) directly from the interface, updating the internal ledger dynamically to match reality.

## C. The M-Pesa Pay Bill Clearing Timing Lag

* The Scenario: On the final day of Term 2, parents pay KSh 150,000 via Pay Bill 7230546. The transactions hit the Safaricom portal immediately, but the funds are not swept into the National Bank account until 48 hours later.
* The V2 Fix: ThorCash introduces an "In-Transit Clearing Account". M-Pesa receipts hit 1020 - M-Pesa Clearing Ledger. When the consolidated bank statement shows the bulk transfer from Safaricom, the reconciliation tool creates a internal transfer journal entry balancing 1020 and moving the funds into the core bank asset account (1010).

## 3. Step-by-Step Reconciliation Workflow
The UI layout for this framework uses an interactive layout that prevents manual alterations to historical logs:
## Step 1: Import Bank Data
The Bursar downloads the official bank statement as a .xlsx or .csv from National Bank and uploads it into the Reconciliation Module.
## Step 2: Automated Match (The First Pass)
The ReconciliationEngine runs an exact-match algorithm on the un-cleared items using two criteria:

* For Deposits: Amount + Student Admission Number (extracted from the transaction reference text code).
* For Withdrawals: Cheque Number / Voucher Reference Number + Exact Amount.
* Matched items are marked with a green checkmark and auto-assigned a status of CLEARED with the statement bank date recorded.

## Step 3: Manual Exception Handling
For entries that fail auto-matching (e.g., a parent paid the correct amount but misspelled the student's name, or bank fees), the interface allows the Bursar to manually pair a cashbook entry to a bank entry or flag it for correction.
## Step 4: Generation of the Bank Reconciliation Statement
Once matching is finalized, the system generates a non-editable PDF Bank Reconciliation Statement structured according to classic accounting principles:

Friends School Kikai Boys Secondary School
Bank Reconciliation Statement As Of Date: 2026-XX-XX
-----------------------------------------------------------
Balance as per Bank Statement:                    KSh XXX,XXX
Add: Receipts in Cashbook not yet in Bank         KSh  XX,XXX  (Deposits in Transit)
Less: Cheques issued not yet presented to Bank   (KSh  XX,XXX) (Unpresented Cheques)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Balance as per Cashbook:                          KSh XXX,XXX
-----------------------------------------------------------
Status: RECONCILED / MATCHED SUCCESS

------------------------------
## 4. Safety Guardrails Against Fraud
To prevent a rogue user from falsifying records to cover up missing funds, the V2 Cashbook framework enforces hard-coded system rules:

   1. Immutable Locking: Once a Cashbook entry is marked as Reconciled in a finalized monthly period, it becomes completely read-only. It cannot be edited, reversed, or deleted under any circumstance.
   2. Date Enforcement: The clearing date of a transaction on the bank statement cannot be earlier than the transaction posting date in the Cashbook. You cannot clear a cheque in the bank before the system records it as written.
   3. Strict Balance-Forward Blocks: The system will physically block the Bursar from opening the reconciliation portal for a new month (e.g., October) if the previous month (September) has an unresolved reconciliation variance greater than zero.
